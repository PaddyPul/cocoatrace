# BetterTrade North Star

Reviewed 2026-10-06. [Execution backlog](DELIVERY_BACKLOG.md), [pilot plan](BETTERTRADE_PILOT_PLAN.md), [architecture](ARCHITECTURE_MODERNIZATION.md).

BetterTrade helps buyers and suppliers source raw materials, evaluate evidence, agree a trade, coordinate protected recorded payments and fulfillment, and retain an auditable material and decision history. It should support domestic, within-region cross-border and inter-region trade. An optional curated logistics service marketplace connects agreed trades to qualified providers through separate service orders; manual external arrangements remain available.

The immediate wedge is a founder-approved narrow invited buyer–supplier pilot; Ghana/EU cocoa is existing context, not a permanent product restriction or proof of every corridor. Conventional inventory needs no fictional farm. Source-traceable/organic claims require appropriate records and evidence. Industrial materials, units, restricted cargo and route-specific documents need explicit policies before expansion.

The company ambition is **USD 8M pre-seed or more**. No technical checklist guarantees valuation. Evidence must combine customer pain, repeated completed trades and time savings, willingness to pay, defensible trust/fulfillment data, transparent unit economics, security/recovery proof and a team/codebase able to develop safely. Investor demonstrations and actual customer access have separate release gates.

BetterTrade coordinates commercial records and decisions. It does not currently hold funds, provide escrow, act as a bank/carrier/customs broker, certify all goods or guarantee legal compliance. Provider verification means a documented scoped review, not universal assurance.

The next technical priorities are security boundary closure, coherent fresh-account journeys and shared trade actions, reliable hosted operations, bounded workloads and automated evidence. Provider onboarding/RFQ/award/fulfillment follows core pilot readiness. Keep the zero-budget constraint visible; if safe real-data hosting is unavailable, continue synthetic demos rather than weakening controls.

Success before pilot: every core gate closed with an owner and evidence. Success before provider launch: core plus provider gates closed and actual reviewed providers. Success toward investment: sustained customer/commercial and operational proof rather than a large feature count. See `npm run backlog:progress` for measured backlog closure, not company valuation.
