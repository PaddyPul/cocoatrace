# Trade currency foundation

Initial supported write policy: EUR, USD, GHS and GBP. Each uses two decimal places for payable amounts. This is an engineering pilot policy pending commercial approval, not a claim of provider availability in every country. Supplier selects currency when publishing. Buyer quotes in the listing currency; changing price does not change currency. No FX or currency preference conversion occurs.

Currency is fixed by the accepted offer and propagated to the contract, payment request and platform fee. Installments inherit the payment request currency. Creation and acceptance reject mismatches before inventory changes. Payment terms, payment mutations, dispatch progress and final settlement reject payment/contract currency drift. Historical data is retained without automatic relabeling or amount changes; an incompatible pending legacy offer needs a replacement quote. Unsupported historical completed currencies remain readable. Refund flows remain unimplemented.

Quantity accepts three decimal places and unit price four. BigInt decimal parsing calculates the product, rounds once half up to cents, and enforces the existing NUMERIC(14,2) range and minimum 0.01 payable trade value. Deposits round half up from the rounded total; the balance is the exact cent remainder. All existing five payment plans use this allocation. The dispatch gate compares exact decimal cents without floating-point tolerance. Fee estimates continue PostgreSQL NUMERIC calculation: round the raw agreed quantity × price × rate/10000 to two decimals, independent of the buyer total. That preserves the seller fee policy and historical comparisons.

Public prices explicitly label currency; buyer offer submissions use the listing currency. Offer tables and accepted deals use database trade/payment totals where available. Estimated pre-submission UI previews and numeric display formatting remain presentation only. Existing compatibility number outputs contain amounts at most twelve integer digits and two decimals, below safe integer-cent bounds; no intermediate floating-point multiplication performs payable allocation.

No database migration, dependency, secret or provider integration is introduced. No amount/currency backfill is performed. Reject mismatches rather than repairing historical finance automatically. Do not enable JPY or three-decimal currencies by simply extending the selector: currency-specific rounding, validation, statement formatting and policy snapshots must be added together. CUR-003 stays open for that work. No FX, tax invoice, refund or new language support is claimed.

Verification: unit tests exercise half cents, range/precision rejection, supported/unsupported currency policy, 4,995 deposit allocations and one-cent dispatch shortfall. PostgreSQL regressions cover all four currencies and five plans, malformed legacy offers and payment drift without mutation. Browser tests submit a GHS offer through the actual UI and exercise GHS fee verification. Native Docker/browser tests must pass before merging.
