# Supply and demand lifecycle

New listings expire30 days after creation by default. Existing legacy listings retain their original no-expiry terms and are labelled accordingly; owners can review and renew them explicitly. Deadlines are enforced by the API/database clock for offers and acceptance. Expired publications are excluded from marketplace supply and reservation budgets, but remain visible in the supplier listing register for review. Direct links/history remain readable, explicitly explain expiry and disable new-offer controls; the server remains authoritative. Partial acceptance carries the original deadline into unsold continuation; it does not grant a fresh term.

Supplier My listings → Review and renew requires a10–1000 character reason and reviewed future expiry within90 days. Renewal requires active, still-owned available inventory, sufficient unreserved quantity and no recall. An expired listing cannot bypass renewal with active=true PATCH. Withdrawn/accepted supply cannot renew. Pending offers on renewal become rejected; accepted deals remain unchanged. A renewal audit records old/new expiry and rejected count, not an unbounded list. Failures retain the explanation in the UI. Inventory and offer locks follow existing recall→holding→listings→offers order.

Buyer saved sourcing briefs have a Sourcing closeout panel. Close without award can end a draft/open request after confirmation/reason. An award requires an open request and a noncancelled same-buyer same-commodity existing trade agreement ID. There is no historical request→offer provenance, so the receipt explicitly records buyer_manual association, not inferred origin, signed terms, full quantity coverage or completed fulfilment. Buyer reviews all allocations before terminal closeout. No automatic close at first accepted offer and no automatic cancellation of unrelated offers/deals. Terminal requirements cannot be edited; a receipt remains available on reopening the brief.

The receipt and status transition/audit commit atomically; repeat/concurrent decisions fail409. The receipt cannot update/delete through ordinary SQL or HTTP. If demand changes later, create a new brief referencing the prior procurement decision; do not rewrite history. Later contract cancellation does not erase historical association or reopen demand automatically.

Forward migrations041/042 run during normal startup. Keep existing project/volumes. No reset or dependency change. Restore behavior via reviewed forward corrections; do not deploy older images blindly against the new ledger. Native/browser runtime acceptance and hosted capacity remain separate gates.
